Our Story

A Recipe Passed Down,
Never Compromised

What began as a humble stall in Penang has grown into a beloved institution — but the recipe has never changed. Pak Din's original dalcha gravy, simmered for hours with whole spices and slow-cooked mutton, remains the heart of everything we serve.

  • Freshly cooked every morning from scratch
  • Only locally-sourced halal ingredients
  • No MSG, no shortcuts, no compromises
  • The same recipe Pak Din used in 1998
