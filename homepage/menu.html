What We Serve

Our Signature Dishes

Every plate is built around the dalcha — a rich, spiced lentil-and-meat gravy that defines our kitchen.

Signature

Nasi Dalcha Kambing

Fluffy basmati rice served with our legendary mutton dalcha gravy, slow-cooked with cardamom, cinnamon and ghee.

Classic

Nasi Dalcha Ayam

Tender free-range chicken simmered in the same heritage dalcha base — a lighter but equally satisfying choice.

Special

Nasi Dalcha Daging

Premium beef cuts braised low and slow until fork-tender, bathed in our aromatic golden dalcha sauce.

Side

Acar Rampah

House-pickled vegetables spiced with turmeric and mustard seeds — the perfect tangy counterpoint to the rich dalcha.

Add-on

Telur Goreng Berempah

Crispy fried egg coated in a fragrant spice crust — a classic Pak Din add-on loved by regulars.

Drink

Teh Tarik Pak Din

Our frothy pulled milk tea — brewed strong, sweetened just right, and pulled to a perfect silky finish.

Pak Din Nasi Dalcha

A family tradition since 1998 — serving authentic, freshly cooked Nasi Dalcha to the people of Penang and beyond.
